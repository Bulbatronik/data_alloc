# Pre-registration: mode competition in SFT→GRPO allocation (v1, 2026-09-28)

Freeze before analysing the Qwen-I rerun and week-1 probes. Existing runs generated the hypotheses, so they are **exploratory**. Confirmatory data: new GRPO replicates, the Qwen-I rerun, MATH. Llama is primary; Qwen-I replicates.

## 0. Facts checked (existing Llama runs, CPU)

- **Three modes, not two.** Untrained Llama under our prompt: 0% native, 76% `####`. Full GRPO ends 90% `####` (52.8). After 50 SFT demos (greedy): native 36.5%, gold 14%, `####` 19%, other 31%. Low-dose SFT mainly *unlocks* the vendor format.
- **"55.4 vs 43.5" is dose-confounded** and pools full-GRPO (`####`) runs into "native". Dose alone predicts final mode with AUC 0.98. The only within-dose contrast (n=100) is 55.7 vs 48.5: Δ7.2, run-level SE 1.35, 3 vs 3.
- **Same after-SFT state, different outcome.** Both seed-43 n=100 adapters (random, adaptive) show 0.5% native, 94–95% gold, 37–38% accuracy; one ends native, one gold.
- **Item-level tests overstate run differences.** Two gold-final runs at one dose differ by 3.6 pts, item-paired SE 1.1.
- **Item discordance** is 12–13% within native, 17–19% within gold, 25–27% across modes.
- **Buckets are not independent.** Random buckets are nested across doses and identical across models. Adaptive buckets share 52–67% of items across seeds. Same-seed methods share `set_seed`.
- **Old Qwen-I data are discarded.** `repetition_penalty=1.1` penalises gold style (`<<a*b=c>>c` repeats numbers), probably in rollouts too.

## 1. Mode classifier

**Primary: reference-policy likelihood (RPL).** Three frozen references per (model, task):

- π_P: untrained model, our prompt.
- π_N: untrained model, vendor-native template (Meta Llama-3 math; Qwen "step by step … \boxed{}").
- π_G: full-SFT gold adapter (n=1000).

Score: ℓ_k = log π_k(y|x_k)/|y|.

- **Label** = argmax ℓ_k if the margin ≥ τ (set on reference samples); otherwise "unassigned".
- **Gold score** g = ℓ_G − max(ℓ_P, ℓ_N).

Why primary: it works on both tasks (MATH has no `<<>>`, and gold MATH traces share `\boxed` with native), it is continuous, and it costs about 0.02 GPU-h per 1319 outputs.

**Robustness: surface rules.**

- GSM8K: `<<>>` → gold; else `\boxed` / `## Step` → native; else `####` → prompt; else other.
- MATH: a logistic model on six fixed features (log length, `## Step` count, "final answer is", `\boxed` position, LaTeX density, newline rate), trained on reference samples.

Embedding clustering (separates by topic) and LLM judges (not reproducible): adjudication only.

**Validation gates (per task):**

- **Hand labels:** 200, two annotators blind to run and dose; half after-SFT samples (doses 50–250), half final outputs, 50 enriched for RPL/surface disagreement. Target κ(RPL, consensus) ≥ 0.80.
- **GSM8K cross-check:** ≥95% of `<<>>` outputs labelled gold.
- **Unassigned:** ≤10%. The surface rule already fails this, with 31% "other" at dose 50.
- **Stripped-annotation test:** delete `<<…>>` and rescore. If ≥50% flip, "gold style" means the annotation, and claims are reframed.

**Run label.** s_m is mode m's share of the greedy test outputs.

- A run ends in m if s_m ≥ 0.8; otherwise it is **mixed**. The 0.5 threshold is a sensitivity analysis.
- Analyses use logit(s_gold).
- **Required:** T=0.8 shares on final adapters (256 test prompts × 4). Greedy fixes the mode at the first tokens, turning graded shares into 0/1 labels, which can fake bistability.

## 2. Hypotheses and tests

**Unit = GRPO run.** Item-level bootstrap is valid only for single-run contrasts.

- **SESOI:** 3 pts.
- **Error control:** fixed sequence C4 → C1c → C2 → C3 per model at α=0.05. Stop at the first failure; the rest become exploratory. C5 is a separate family. Other analyses use BH at q=0.10, labelled exploratory.
- **Models:**
  - Run level: acc ~ factor(dose) + gold + seed (fixed).
  - Item level: GLMM correct ~ factor(dose) + gold + (1|item) + (1|run).
  - p-values by parametric-bootstrap likelihood-ratio test or within-dose run permutation.

| Claim | H0 | Test | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1a | Gold share after SFT does not rise with dose | Jonckheere–Terpstra | p<0.05 (descriptive) |
| C1b | ≤70% of runs commit | Binomial test on runs with max s_m ≥ 0.8, under greedy and T=0.8 | ≥85% commit |
| C1c | β_gold = 0 given dose | Both models above; partial R² of mode given dose, over doses with both modes | β ≤ −3 with CI excluding 0; partial R² ≥ 0.5; residual SD ≤ 1.2 × rerun SD (1.7). No cross-dose marginal contrasts |
| C2 | Transition is graded (unimodal final share) | At n*, K ≥ 10 runs with fresh buckets and pools; Welch and permutation tests on the basin gap | ≥2 runs per basin, ≤1 mixed, gap ≥ 3 pts |
| C3 | Adds nothing beyond dose | S = logit(ŵ₀) + c·Δ̂·steps, c fit leave-one-dose-out. Brier skill score (BSS) vs a dose-only logistic, seed-cluster bootstrap | BSS ≥ 0.2 with CI > 0; within-dose AUC ≥ 0.8 on ≥ 8 vs 8 runs; pre-registered n* for Qwen-I rerun and MATH within ×1.5 |
| C4 | Prompting recovers native-final accuracy | (a) Untrained model, Meta template, 0-shot greedy. (b) Full GRPO under the Meta template, 3 seeds | Kill if (a) ≥ 54.8 (native-final 56.3 − 1.5) or (b) ≥ 55.1 (SFT-50 final − 1.5) |
| C5 | No step, or no n* shift, on MATH | Gate: RPL separates π_G from π_N with AUC ≥ 0.95, else "untestable". Then C1c, within-gold slope on log(N−n), and n* from logistic P(gold) ~ log n | All pass; sign of log(n*_MATH/n*_GSM8K) matches theory, CI excluding 0 |

**Against the planned AUC ≥ 0.8 gate:**

- Only the 6 runs at n=100 are not determined by dose; on all runs, dose alone scores 0.98.
- At 3 vs 3 under H0, P(AUC ≥ 0.8) = 0.10.
- Hanley–McNeil 95% CIs: AUC 0.89 at 3 vs 3 → [0.59, 1]; AUC 0.8 at 10 vs 10 → [0.60, 1]. A lower bound above 0.70 needs ~40 runs per class.
- **C2 and C3 conflict.** If one adapter yields both basins, within-dose AUC is capped near 0.5, and C3 must be judged by calibration of P(escape).

## 3. Power

Sample-size formulas, with mode ICC ρ_w ≈ 0.3 and correctness ICC ρ_c ≈ 0.5:

- SE(ŵ) = √[w(1−w)(1+(m−1)ρ_w)/(Pm)]
- SE(p̂_m) ≈ √[p(1−p)(1+7wρ_c)/(wPm)]

Values at 256 × 8:

| w₀ | SE(ŵ) (relative) | SE(p̂), p=0.3 | SE(p̂), p=0.6 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.01 | 0.0039 (39%) | 0.103 | 0.110 |
| 0.05 | 0.0085 (17%) | 0.049 | 0.053 |
| 0.10 | 0.0117 (12%) | 0.037 | 0.040 |
| 0.20 | 0.0156 (8%) | 0.030 | 0.032 |
| 0.50 | 0.0195 (4%) | 0.024 | 0.025 |

256 × 8 estimates w₀ ≥ 0.05. It **cannot** resolve Δ = p_nat − p_gold ≈ 0.05–0.10 when w₀ ≤ 0.1, yet the critical regime is w₀ ≈ 0.5–5%. Pooled per-mode means are also prompt-confounded.

**Fixes:**

1. At critical doses, sample all 900 bucket prompts × 8: SE(ŵ) ≈ 0.003 at w = 0.02.
2. Estimate p_m with mode-forced prefixes on 256 prompts × 8 per mode. The within-prompt SE(Δ) is about 0.015–0.02 regardless of w₀.
3. Validate forcing at dose 50: forced vs natural p_m within 0.05.

**Bistability replicates.** P(at least 2 runs per basin with K = 10) is 0.98, 0.85 and 0.62 at q = 0.5, 0.3 and 0.2. Three seeds never suffice.

**Mechanism.** Rerun GRPO only from the 6 existing n=100 adapters, 2 new seeds each (12 runs).

- If every adapter repeats its basin: p = 2.4×10⁻⁴ under ICC = 0.
- Adapter ICC ≥ 0.7: the outcome is set by the initial condition.
- Adapter ICC ≤ 0.3: bistability is noise-driven.

## 4. Confounds and controls

| Threat | Control |
|---|---|
| Mode is collinear with dose | Within-dose inference only; dense doses near n* |
| "Seed" bundles bucket, LoRA init, data order and rollout RNG | Add `--grpo_seed`; crossed design at n*: 2 buckets × 2 SFT seeds × 2 GRPO seeds. Does the same random bucket push Llama and Qwen alike? |
| Fixed `data_seed`: GRPO buckets overlap about 90% | Resample the pool (3 data_seeds) for confirmatory C2 |
| Adaptive buckets are correlated | Confirmatory inference uses random buckets only |
| Metastability (≈112 steps, lr 2e-5, β=0) | +1 epoch on the gold-final n=100 runs; if one escapes, C2 becomes escape time |
| 512-token cap penalises the long native mode | Log truncation by mode; use 1024 near n* |
| Grader leniency depends on mode (`####` first, last-number fallback) | Strict per-marker grader plus math-verify; the gap must keep ≥80% of its size |
| Qwen2.5 contamination | Evaluate finals on GSM-Plus and GSM-Symbolic |
| MATH numeric filter drops expression answers | Report and stratify by subject; math-verify on full L1–3 |
| On MATH, gold shares `\boxed` with native; lower p compresses Δ | Separability gate; more seeds near n* |

## 5. MATH replication

The plan (6 doses × 3 seeds × 2 models): 24.6 GPU-h GRPO (4100 prompts per seed-model) + ~5 eval, **≈30 GPU-h**. **Insufficient:**

- The grid only brackets n*, so a shift under about 2.5× is invisible.
- Three seeds per dose cannot show bistability.
- Quasi-separation makes the CI on n* unbounded.

**Minimal fix:**

- **Stage A (≈3 GPU-h):** SFT-only adapters at {25, 50, 100, 150, 250, 500} × 3, then the §3 probes. The theory's n* is appended here before Stage B.
- **Stage B:** GRPO at {0, 500, 1000} × 3 seeds, plus {n*/2, n*/√2, n*, √2·n*, 2n*} with 5 seeds on the central three and 3 on the outer two.
  - For n* = 150: 22k prompts, about 22 GPU-h for the 1B model and 13 for the 0.5B.
  - Eval: about 9 GPU-h.
- **Total ≈47 GPU-h.**

## 7. Teacher-trace arm (registered 2026-10-01, before any GRPO on teacher-trace adapters)

Setup:
- Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct, SFT on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct traces (`runs/gsm8k/teachers/qwen7b.jsonl`, first correct trace per problem), SFT lr 1e-4.
- Then 1-epoch GRPO with the main-curve settings; full test set (1319).
- Doses n = 50, 100, 250, 500; seeds 42, 43, 44.
- w0 = exact P("## Step") on the GRPO prompts, measured on the same SFT adapters the GRPO starts from.

Known before registering (seed-42 SFT-only scan, 300-item eval):
- w0 = 1.1% (n=50), 0.32% (n=100), 8.5e-6 (n=250);
- after-SFT accuracy 46 / 50 / 56 / 52 vs untrained 45.

Predictions:
- **T1 (threshold, necessary condition).** Every run with w0 < 1% ends with < 10% native ("## Step") greedy answers.
- **T2 (band).** Runs with w0 between 1% and 1.3% (expected at n=50) escape in at most 1 of 3 seeds. No prediction for w0 > 3%.
- **T3 (no cliff with a good teacher).** Mean final accuracy at n=250 ≥ 54.0, which is above full GRPO (52.7) and at least 6 points above gold traces at n=250 (46.4). Mean final accuracy at n=500 ≥ 50.
- **T4 (update budget).** The w0 1% crossing lies in n·η ∈ (0.005, 0.0125]: w0(n=100) < 1% for all 3 seeds.

Scoring: T1/T4 per run; T2/T3 on seed means. Any T1 violation (an escape from w0 < 1%) counts against the threshold claim.

**Outcome (scored 2026-10-01, `runs/theory/scratch/score_teacher_prereg.py`): 4/4 held.**

| Prediction | Result | Holds? |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | 9 of 9 runs with w0 < 1% ended with 0% native | yes |
| T2 | the one run in the 1–1.3% band (s42 n=50, 1.2%) did not escape | yes |
| T3 | n=250: 55.2 ± 0.5; n=500: 54.5 ± 0.7 | yes |
| T4 | w0(n=100) = 0.24–0.35% for all seeds | yes |

Not registered:
- Above 3%, the two n=50 runs (w0 3.9% and 4.4%) split: one escaped to native (57.0), one stayed in prose (52.0). So "reliable escape above ~3%" from the gold runs does not hold here; ≳1% is necessary, not sufficient.

Final accuracy by dose (mean of 3 seeds):

| n | Teacher traces | Gold traces |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (full GRPO) | 52.7 | 52.7 |
| 50 | 53.4 | 56.6 |
| 100 | 54.6 | 51.2 |
| 250 | 55.2 | 46.4 |
| 500 | 54.5 | 44.0 |

With teacher traces the curve is flat (54.5–55.2 for n = 100–500). With gold traces it has a cliff.

## 8. What limits RL recovery: sampling vs cumulative update (registered 2026-10-01, before running)

**Adapters.** Two existing gold-trace SFT adapters (Llama-1B) that did *not* escape under the standard GRPO (G=8, lr 2e-5, 1 epoch):

| Name | Source | w0 |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | lr5e-5 scan, seed 42, n=250 | 0.39% |
| A2 | lr1e-4 scan, seed 43, n=125 | 1.05% |

**Arms.** Same GRPO bucket, 1 epoch, same number of optimizer steps:

| Arm | Change | GRPO seeds |
|---|---|---|
| G8 | control | 1 |
| G32 | group size 32: 4× rollouts per step, same Ση | 1, 2 |
| LR2x | GRPO lr 4e-5: same rollouts, 2× Ση | 1, 2 |

Escape = final greedy native share > 50%.

**Hypotheses.**

| | Sampling-limited (Kimura-type) | Update-limited (drift model Mγ in extinction_theory.md) |
|---|---|---|
| G32 | escapes in ≥ 3/4 | ≤ 1/4 |
| LR2x | ≤ 1/4 | ≥ 2/4 |

**Registered prediction (Mγ):**
- LR2x escapes in ≥ 2/4;
- G32 escapes in ≤ 1/4;
- G8 controls escape in 0/2.

**Reading the outcome:**
- Both ≤ 1/4: the threshold is robust to 4× samples and 2× update at w0 ≤ 1%.
- Both ≥ 3/4: budget-limited, but this test cannot separate the two mechanisms.

## 10. Is the gold cliff only an RL-budget artifact? (registered 2026-10-01, before running)

Setup:
- Llama-1B gold curve at GRPO lr 4e-5 (2×): n = 0 (full GRPO), 100, 250.
- Seeds 42, 43; everything else standard.

Predictions:
- **B1.** Gold n=250 at 2× LR still ends gold-style: final native share < 10% in both seeds.
- **B2.** The cliff shrinks but survives: mean[full GRPO, 2×] − mean[gold n=250, 2×] ≥ 2.0 points. At the standard budget it was 6.3.
- **B3.** No prediction for n=100 (w0 1.3–3%, stochastic band).

Reading the outcome:
- If B2 fails (gap < 2), the allocation cliff at n=250 is mostly an RL-budget artifact. The paper then claims only the style-recovery bonus (~3 points), not a cliff.

**§10 outcome (scored 2026-10-01): B1 and B2 held.**

Final accuracy at GRPO lr 4e-5:

| n | Seed 42 | Seed 43 | Mean | Final style |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (full GRPO) | 53.1 | 56.9 | 55.0 | prompt prose |
| 100 | 56.7 | 57.3 | 57.0 | native in both seeds (vs 1/3 at the standard LR) |
| 250 | 47.2 | 49.2 | 48.2 | native 0%, gold 88–93% |

- **B1 holds:** gold n=250 stays gold-style.
- **B2 holds:** the gap is 6.8, against 6.3 at the standard budget.

The cliff is not an RL-budget artifact; a larger budget raises the whole curve, and the stochastic band (n=100) resolves toward recovery. The 51–53 within-gold-style results in §8 came from adapters SFT'd with a smaller update (A1 used lr 5e-5).
